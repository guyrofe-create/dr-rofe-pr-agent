Reputation Agent Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

Reputation Agent is a reputation-management and publishing platform. This policy explains how the service handles information when a user connects an account, reviews content, publishes approved content, or uses monitoring and analytics features.

Information we process

  • Account identifiers and basic profile information supplied by connected platforms.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to perform authorized actions. We do not ask users to provide their social-media passwords.
  • Content, drafts, publishing instructions, public posts, public reviews, and public search results used by the service.
  • Performance and analytics information made available by connected platforms.
  • Technical logs needed for security, reliability, auditing, and troubleshooting.

How we use information

We use information only to provide requested features, including account connection, reputation monitoring, analytics, content preparation, approval workflows, authorized publishing, error reporting, and service security. Reputation Agent does not sell personal information.

Connected services

The service may connect to providers such as LinkedIn, Google, YouTube, Meta, and other publishing or analytics platforms. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Reputation Agent requests only the permissions needed for the features a user enables.

Sharing

Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers solely to operate the platform, comply with law, prevent abuse, or protect users and the service. We do not share connected-account data for independent advertising purposes.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain an audit trail, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. A user may disconnect a platform through that platform's account settings and may request deletion of stored account information by contacting us.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including restricted access to credentials and encrypted transport. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

User choices

Users control which accounts they connect and which permissions they grant. Permissions may be withdrawn from the relevant platform's security or connected-app settings. Withdrawal may disable related Reputation Agent features.

Children

Reputation Agent is a business service and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
